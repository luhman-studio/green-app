Where it comes from

Read it left to right: what causes the emissions (the fuel, the electricity, the farm, the factory) → what you do with it → which area it belongs to → your whole footprint. Every band’s height is exactly its tonnes and the columns have no gaps, so the picture is the arithmetic — which means a small thing looks small. Bands too thin to hold a label don’t get one; hover or click them instead. The dashed line is the 1.5 °C budget for 2030. Ticking a change below redraws everything. Hover to follow a path from left to right, click to hold it and see more.

    How the math works

    Everything runs in your browser — no server, no AI. Start with Method & sources: it explains every part of the calculation and where each number comes from. The rest is the actual JavaScript that runs, which you can also open in the js/ and data/ folders. Check it yourself with node tests/engine.test.js (hand-calculated examples) and node tests/audit.js (3,000 random profiles).
